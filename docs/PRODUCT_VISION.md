# Product Vision

FinSentiMind is a hybrid investment intelligence workstation for personal,
read-only equity research on IDX.

It combines:

1. Market monitoring and information density.
2. Quantitative research and explainable analytics.
3. Fundamental research and valuation.
4. Capital-flow context.
5. Portfolio tracking and risk analysis.

## Principles

- Evidence over assumptions.
- Explainability over black-box output.
- Data integrity over feature quantity.
- Risk awareness over return promises.
- Research quality over visual decoration.

## Boundaries

- No live order execution, broker routing, custody, or automated trading.
- No fabricated fundamentals, flows, macro data, or predictions.
- ML research remains `NOT_VALIDATED`.
- Public launch requires separate legal, licensing, security, and operational
  approval.

## User experience

The terminal must explain what a metric means, how it is calculated, what data
it needs, what assumptions it makes, and when it should not be trusted.

The product is an educational and analytical workstation, not a guarantee of
investment returns.
